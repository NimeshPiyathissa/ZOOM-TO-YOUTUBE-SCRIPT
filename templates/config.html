Troubleshooting Google's sign-in prompts

"This browser or app may not be secure" - Google sometimes shows this for automated-looking browser profiles. Retry, and if it persists, use the dedicated bot account's recovery options or an App Password if 2FA is on.

Extra verification / "Verify it's you" - complete it interactively over Remote GUI like any normal sign-in; nothing here can skip it, by design.

Session persistence: Zoom/Chrome keep this sign-in in zoombot's home directory (permissions locked to that user only) across restarts. How long it lasts before needing to sign in again isn't something Zoom or Google publish a fixed number for - expect it to survive routine restarts but to eventually need re-authentication, especially after a password change or extended idle period.
