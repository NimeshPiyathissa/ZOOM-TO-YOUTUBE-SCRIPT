Red redesign - pick a direction

A · Oxblood - deep wine-crimson on warm near-black / warm paper, champagne as the single accent, glassy cards, a glowing indicator bar on the sidebar. Calm, expensive, editorial.  B · Signal - one confident vivid-red slab for the sidebar and hero, cool graphite / clean white everywhere else, ice-blue accent. Louder, more broadcast-control-room.

LIVE is never the brand red. In A, LIVE is an orange-leaning scarlet pill with dark text and a pulsing dot; in B it is a black tally pill with a ringing orange dot. Failed is an outlined chip with a cross icon, errors carry a warning triangle, success is green, warnings amber - each also differs by label and motion, so none depends on hue alone. Type: Inter (Black / ExtraBold headings and numbers, tight tracking) + Geist Mono for logs and stats.
